    The Pearl of the Indian Ocean

    Discover
    Sri Lanka

    Sri Lanka is a tropical island of extraordinary contrast: ancient kingdoms and sacred temples, misty mountain roads and world-famous tea estates, golden beaches and dramatic wildlife reserves. It is a place where heritage, nature and warm hospitality come together in a way that feels deeply personal.

    Why travellers return

    Sri Lanka balances iconic sights with a slower pace, making it ideal for travellers who want both cultural depth and natural beauty.

    8 UNESCO Sites, including Sigiriya, Galle Fort and Kandy.
    2,500+ Years of history shaped by kings, kingdoms and sacred traditions.
    1,000+ Tea estates produce some of the world’s finest Ceylon teas.
    30+ National parks and sanctuaries for safaris, birdlife and rainforest encounters.
